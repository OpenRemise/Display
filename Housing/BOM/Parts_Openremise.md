# Necessary parts for housing "Open Remise"

## Bottom part

- 4 × screws (for plastic / PCB), 3 × 6 mm

## Cover

- 4 × screws (for plastic), M3 × 6 mm
- Cutting a thread might be a good idea
- 3 × lightguides, 2 mm diameter, approx. 20 mm long

## Mounting

- To mount the housing on a surface you can use 3 mm screws of your choice.

Note: Measurements are approximate.
